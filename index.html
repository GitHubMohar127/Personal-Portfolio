About

Who I am

I'm a third-year Computer Science & Engineering (AI-ML) student at Sister Nivedita University, under the Techno India Group, originally from Panchal, Bankura, in West Bengal. I work across Python, JavaScript, SQL, and data analytics tools to build systems that go beyond a notebook — models that get wrapped in an interface someone can actually use.

Most of what I build sits at the intersection of machine learning and practical software: a spam classifier trained on real email data, a face-recognition attendance system with a Streamlit dashboard, a medicine-lookup platform, recommendation and prediction models, and Power BI dashboards for sales and social data. I care about getting the full pipeline working — data in, model trained, result usable — rather than stopping at a proof of concept.

Outside of coursework, I'm involved in my college's technical community as a member of the SKEPSIS technical committee and the GeeksforGeeks SNU chapter, and I write about tech on Medium.
